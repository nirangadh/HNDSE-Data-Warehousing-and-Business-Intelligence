Glossary

Every word the module teaches, filed under the day that first uses it.

Day 1

OLTP

Online transaction processing. A system that records events one row at a time, with insert, update, delete and look-up. Each event it records is called a transaction. Thara example: The registration desk adds one enrollment for a student at the counter. That is OLTP work.

OLAP

Online analytical processing. Work that reads many rows at once and groups them to count, sum and compare. Thara example: Counting the enrollments in each course for one term is OLAP work.

workload

The kind of work a database does most of the time: many small writes, or large reads over history. Thara example: The registration system's workload is the desk's small writes, all day, during enrollment week.

data warehouse

A separate system that holds history for analysis. It is subject-oriented, integrated, time-variant and non-volatile. Thara example: The institute's warehouse will answer the Dean's questions without slowing the registration desk.

subject-oriented

Organised around the subject people ask about, not around the screens of the system that records it. Thara example: The warehouse is organised around enrollment, the subject the Dean asks about.

integrated

Data from every source is stored with one spelling for each value and one key for each thing it describes. Thara example: A student keeps one key in the warehouse, whether the row came from the registration system or the admissions registry.

time-variant

Every row carries the time it belongs to, so one period can be compared with another. Thara example: Each enrollment carries its semester and academic year, so Semester 1 of 2024/2025 can be compared with Semester 1 of 2023/2024.

non-volatile

Rows are loaded and read, not edited, so a report gives the same answer next month. Thara example: An enrollment loaded into the warehouse is never changed in place by the desk.

data

A recorded fact about one event, with no context around it. Thara example: Student 3003 enrolled in Web Development in Semester 1 of 2024/2025.

information

Data with context, so it can be read and compared. Thara example: Database Systems had 9 enrollments in Semester 1 of 2024/2025, more than any other course.

knowledge

A pattern you can act on, found by reading information over time and in context. Thara example: Database Systems grew from 3 enrollments in Semester 1 of 2023/2024 to 9 a year later, so demand is growing.

decision support system

A system that helps people move from stored data to a decision, by asking their own questions of it. Thara example: The warehouse and its dashboards help the Dean decide whether to open another intake.
