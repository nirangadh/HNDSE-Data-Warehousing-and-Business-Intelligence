Data Warehousing and Business Intelligence

BSc (Hons) Computing, Level 4, and Higher National Diploma in Software Engineering

Seven teaching days with portable tools, plus a review session.

Scan to open this site on your phone

Over seven teaching days you build one data warehouse, one piece at a time, with portable tools that need no installation: PostgreSQL, DBeaver, pandas in Jupyter, and Metabase. Every lab adds a numbered component to the same warehouse, for the Thara Institute of Computing, a fictional institute in Sri Lanka. On Day 7 you build a second warehouse in a new domain, on your own.
